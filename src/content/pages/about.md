This page is where a visitor decides the company is real. Keep the name, phone, and city identical to the footer and the contact page.

## Who does the work?

Add the owner or the crew in `ownerName`. Leave it blank until you have a name you can stand behind. Do not invent a person for a client site.

## Where is the company based?

The sample address is 100 Main Street, Example City, CA 90000. Change it in `src/config/business.ts` and the footer, schema, and contact page update together.

The cities on the [service areas page](/service-areas/) should match the places this company will actually drive to. The [drain cleaning page](/services/drain-cleaning/) shows how a single job is explained.
