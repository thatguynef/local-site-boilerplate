A slow sink and a backed-up floor drain are different jobs. Tell us which fixture failed and how long it has been happening so the visit matches the problem.

## What does a drain cleaning visit include?

The visit covers the fixture you named and a check of whether the clog is in the branch line or the main line. We confirm the scope by phone before the appointment. We do not start work until you have agreed to that scope.

## What should you do before we arrive?

Keep using other drains only if they are still flowing. If water is covering a floor, stop using the fixtures on that line and call (555) 010-0199.

If you are in another part of the city, start with the [Example City service area](/service-areas/example-city/). More questions are on the [FAQ](/faq/).
